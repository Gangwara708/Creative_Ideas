How we work

Five commitments we will not trade away

🚫

No institute money

We accept no commission, referral fee or "admission incentive" from any college, university, coaching centre or lender. Ever.

📉

No guarantees

We do not promise ranks, seats, visas or jobs, because nobody can honestly promise those. We promise the quality of the analysis.

🔍

Sources on screen

Cutoffs, fee structures and placement reports are opened and read with you in the session. You can verify everything we say.

🔐

Your data stays yours

We never sell or share contact details, and we never ask for your exam portal passwords or OTPs.

🙅

We will turn work down

If a paid session would not meaningfully change your decision, we say so on the free call and send you away.

🗣️

Plain language

Hindi or English, no jargon, and no talking over a 16-year-old's head while only the parents are addressed.

Straight answers

Things consultancies usually don't put on their website

Where our numbers come from

Any statistic on this site — students counselled, ratings, outcomes — must be backed by our own records and is stated as of the date shown. We do not publish "success rates" for admissions or placements, because the honest denominator is impossible to define and the number would be marketing, not information.

What we are not qualified to do

We are not doctors, lawyers, licensed clinical psychologists, financial advisers or immigration agents. When your question belongs to one of those professions, the right answer from us is a referral — and we would rather lose the fee than pretend otherwise.

How we handle disagreement

If a family and a student want different things, we do not pick a side. We put the evidence for each path on the table, name the trade-offs out loud, and let the decision be made by the people who have to live with it.

Our fees, and why

Published openly on the pricing page, with no hidden "processing" charges. If a service is not right for you, the discovery call ends with a no and costs you nothing.
