How it works

A clear process, start to finish

No endless follow-ups, no mystery about what happens next. Four steps, and you leave with something written down.

Our promise: if we don't think we can add value to your situation on the discovery call, we will tell you so and point you somewhere better — at no charge.
Start with the free call

  1. Free discovery call (15 min)

    You explain the situation, we ask the questions nobody has asked yet, and we agree on whether to proceed.

  2. Assessment & homework

    Psychometric test, marks/résumé review, and a short questionnaire for you (and your parents, where relevant).

  3. Counselling session (60–90 min)

    We go through the real options together — with cutoffs, fees, timelines and salary data on the screen, not from memory.

  4. Written plan + follow-up

    A document with Plan A, Plan B and deadlines. Follow-up calls through the admission or application season.

Questions

Quick answers

Do you guarantee admission or a job?
No — and be careful with anyone who does. Admissions depend on your score and the counselling authority; jobs depend on the employer. What we guarantee is honest analysis, a documented plan, and that we will tell you when an option is unrealistic.
Is the first call really free?
Yes. The 15-minute discovery call costs nothing and carries no obligation. We use it to understand your case and tell you whether a paid session would actually help.
Are sessions online or in person?
Both. Most clients choose video calls (Google Meet or Zoom). In-person sessions are available at our {{ site.contact.city }} office by appointment.
Will you contact my college or fill my form for me?
We guide you through choice filling and forms and review everything before you submit — but you (or your parent) always submit from your own login. We never ask for your passwords.
