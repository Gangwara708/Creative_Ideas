The fine print, up front

What is and isn't included

QuestionAnswer
Is the discovery call really free?Yes. 15 minutes, no card details, no obligation.
When do I pay?After the discovery call, before the paid session is scheduled.
How do I pay?UPI, bank transfer or card via our payment link. A GST invoice is issued for every payment.
Do you take cash?Only for in-person sessions, against a receipt.
Can I reschedule?Yes, free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice. See the refund policy.
Can I get a refund?Full refund before the first paid session begins. After that it is prorated — full terms in the refund policy.
Are college or exam fees included?No. You pay all third-party fees (application, counselling registration, tests) directly to those bodies.
Do you offer EMI?Not currently. Larger packages can be split into two instalments on request.
Is there any concession?Yes — a limited number of subsidised places each month for students in genuine financial need. Ask on the call; no documents needed beyond an honest conversation.
