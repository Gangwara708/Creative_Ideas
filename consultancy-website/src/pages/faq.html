About the service

How we work

Do you guarantee admission, a seat, a visa or a job?
No. Admissions are decided by counselling authorities and universities on the basis of your score, category and choices; visas are decided by governments; jobs are decided by employers. Anyone who guarantees these outcomes for a fee is either misinformed or misleading you. What we are accountable for is the quality of the analysis, the honesty of the options list, and the plan you leave with.
Is the first call really free, or is it a sales call?
It is genuinely free and it lasts about 15 minutes. Its purpose is to understand your situation and tell you whether a paid session would change your decision. Sometimes the honest answer is "no, you already know what to do" — and that is how the call ends.
Are sessions online or in person?
Both. Most clients across India choose video (Google Meet or Zoom); in-person sessions run at our {{ site.contact.city }} office by appointment. Quality is identical — the assessment and the written plan are the same either way.
Which languages do you counsel in?
Hindi and English, and we switch freely mid-sentence if that is how you think.
How long does the whole process take?
A single session is one hour. The student guidance pack runs two to three weeks including the assessment. Admission-season support runs as long as the counselling rounds do.
Fees

Money

What does it cost?
Every fee is published on the pricing page. You get a written quote after the free call, before you pay anything.
Do you take commission from colleges or universities?
No. Not from colleges, coaching institutes, universities, education-loan agents or overseas institutions. Our only income is the fee our client pays, and that is the structural reason our advice can be independent.
Can I get a refund?
Yes, on the terms set out in our refund and cancellation policy — in short, a full refund before the first paid session starts, and a prorated refund for unused sessions in a package.
Is there any help if we cannot afford the fee?
We keep a small number of subsidised places each month for students in genuine financial need. Please just say so on the call — you will not be asked to prove it with paperwork.
Privacy & safety

Your information

What do you do with my data?
We use it to contact you and run your counselling, and for nothing else. We do not sell, rent or share contact details with colleges, coaching centres or marketers. Full detail is in the privacy policy, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time.
My child is under 18. Can they book a session?
A parent or legal guardian must make the booking and give consent, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We will speak with the student one-to-one during the session — with that consent in place.
Will you ask for my exam portal login?
Never. No legitimate consultant needs your password or OTP. We guide you while you operate your own login, and we review the list before you lock it.
Are counselling sessions confidential?
Yes. Notes are private and are not shared with family, schools or employers without your written consent — except where the law requires disclosure or there is a serious, immediate risk to someone's safety.
Suitability

Is this for me?

My score is very low. Is there any point?
Usually yes — low-score situations are exactly where families know the fewest options and are most likely to be sold something expensive. But if we cannot add value, we will tell you on the free call.
I am 35 and want to change careers. Am I too old?
No, but the strategy is different from a fresher's: it leans on repositioning existing experience rather than starting over. That is precisely what the career consulting engagement is built for.
I think I need a therapist, not a career counsellor.
Then that is what you should have, and we will say so. We provide non-clinical coaching only. For immediate support in India, Tele-MANAS is available 24×7 on 14416.
My parents and I disagree. Can you settle it?
We will not take a side, but we will put the evidence for each path on the table and name the trade-offs plainly. In most families, the argument was never really about facts — but facts make it much easier to end.
