1. Cancelling before the service starts

Write to {{ site.contact.email }} before your first paid session begins and you get a full refund, no reason required.

2. Rescheduling

Life happens. Tell us at least 24 hours before your slot and we will move it at no cost. With less notice we will still accommodate one reschedule; after that the slot is counted as delivered, because the counsellor's time was reserved and could not be offered to someone else.

3. Packages part-way through

If you stop a multi-session package, we refund the fee for sessions not yet delivered, calculated at the published single-session rate, minus any third-party cost we have already paid on your behalf (for example a psychometric assessment licence). We will show you the calculation in writing before processing.

4. What is not refundable

  • Sessions already delivered.
  • Assessment licences already activated in your name.
  • Fees paid by you directly to third parties — examination bodies, counselling authorities, universities or platforms. Their own refund rules apply.
  • Dissatisfaction with the content of honest advice. We are paid for analysis, not for agreeing with a preferred conclusion. If we did not deliver what was described, that is a different matter and we will make it right.

5. Outcomes are never a refund ground

We do not guarantee admissions, seats, ranks, scholarships, visas or jobs, and we say so before you pay. Not receiving one of these is therefore not a basis for a refund.

6. How to request a refund

  1. Email {{ site.contact.email }} with your name, invoice number and reason.
  2. We acknowledge within 2 working days and confirm the amount.
  3. Approved refunds are initiated within 7 working days.
  4. The money reaches your original payment method typically within 5–10 working days after that, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Refunds are always returned to the original payment method. We do not refund to a different account.

7. If you are unhappy

Tell us first — most problems are fixable and we would rather fix them. Write to {{ site.contact.supportEmail }} and we will respond within 7 working days. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 are unaffected by anything in this policy.
