This policy explains how {{ site.legalName }} ("we", "us") handles personal data collected through {{ site.url }} and in the course of providing counselling services. We act as the Data Fiduciary for this data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. What we collect

  • Contact details you submit: name, mobile number, email, city.
  • Enquiry details: who you are, the service you need, and anything you write in the message box.
  • Counselling information shared during an engagement: academic records, exam scores, resumes, assessment responses and session notes.
  • Payment records: invoice and transaction references. Card and bank credentials are handled by our payment gateway and are never stored by us.
  • Technical data: if analytics is enabled, aggregated page-visit data. We do not use advertising trackers or sell audience data.

2. Why we use it

  • To contact you about the enquiry you submitted.
  • To deliver the counselling service you have engaged us for.
  • To issue invoices and meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • To improve our services in aggregate, without identifying individuals.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not pass your contact details to colleges, coaching institutes, universities, lenders or marketing agencies.

3. Legal basis and consent

We process personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you submit a form or sign an engagement, and for certain legitimate uses permitted by law. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to {{ site.contact.email }}. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, and may mean we can no longer provide the service.

4. Children's data

Many of the students we advise are under 18. For any person under 18 we require the verifiable consent of a parent or legal guardian before processing their data, and we do not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children. A parent or guardian may ask us to access, correct or delete their child's data at any time.

5. Sharing with service providers

We share the minimum necessary data with processors who help us operate, such as:

  • Website hosting and form-handling providers
  • Email, calendar and video-conferencing providers
  • Payment gateway and accounting providers
  • Psychometric assessment platforms, where an assessment is part of your service

We disclose data to anyone else only where the law requires it, or where there is a serious and immediate risk to someone's safety.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become engagements: up to 12 months.
  • Client records and session notes: up to 3 years after the engagement ends.
  • Invoices and financial records: as long as tax law requires (currently at least 6 years for GST records; confirm the applicable period with your accountant).

We delete or anonymise data once these periods lapse, or earlier on request.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may:

  • Ask what personal data of yours we hold and how it is processed
  • Ask us to correct or complete inaccurate data
  • Ask us to erase data we no longer need
  • Withdraw consent
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf
  • Raise a grievance with us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved

Write to {{ site.contact.email }} and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards: encrypted transport (HTTPS), access limited to counsellors who need it, two-factor authentication on business accounts, and no storage of card or banking credentials. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as required.

9. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies. If website analytics is enabled, it records aggregate visit statistics. Your browser settings can block these at any time without affecting your ability to use the site.
