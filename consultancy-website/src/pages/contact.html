Before you write in

Three things that make the first call better

Bring the numbers

Score, percentile, rank, category, domicile state, board marks, or your current CTC and notice period — whichever applies.

Bring the constraint

Budget ceiling, cities you cannot move to, family expectations. Constraints shape advice more than ambitions do.

Bring the real question

Not "what should I do", but "should I do A or B, and why". A sharper question gets a sharper answer.
