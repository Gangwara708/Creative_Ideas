When the question is bigger than a course or a job

"Samajh nahi aa raha aage kya karna hai." It is one of the most common sentences we hear, and one of the least well served. This is structured, private, judgement-free work on direction — over four sessions, not four years.

What this is, and what it is not. This is non-clinical coaching: goal-setting, decision-making and accountability. It is not therapy, psychiatric treatment or a diagnosis of any condition. If what you are carrying is clinical — persistent low mood, anxiety that stops daily functioning, trauma, self-harm — you deserve a licensed professional, and we will help you find one.

Who comes to us for this

  • Students who cleared an exam, joined a course, and feel nothing about it
  • Graduates a year out, watching batchmates move while they stall
  • Professionals who are successful on paper and hollow in practice
  • People after a big rupture — a failure, a loss, a layoff, a move
  • Anyone who keeps starting plans on Monday and abandoning them by Thursday

The four sessions

  1. Where you actually are

    An honest inventory: current commitments, finances, energy, constraints, and the things you have been avoiding naming.

  2. What you are built for

    Strengths, values and motivation mapping — what has genuinely engaged you in the past, and what reliably drains you.

  3. Options, narrowed

    From a fog of possibilities to three real options, each with its cost, its first step, and how you would know within 90 days whether it is working.

  4. The 90-day plan

    Written, specific, small enough to start this week. Plus two fortnightly check-in calls, because plans die in week three without one.

What makes it different from advice from friends

Nobody in the room needs an outcome

Your family loves you and has preferences. Your friends are comparing themselves to you. A counsellor has neither problem.

Structure, not sympathy

Sympathy feels good for an hour. A written decision framework survives the week.

Complete confidentiality

Sessions are private. Nothing is shared with family, employers or anyone else without your written consent.

Accountability that is kind

Check-ins are for course-correcting, not for making you feel guilty about a bad fortnight.

If you need immediate mental-health support in India: Tele-MANAS, the Government of India's national mental-health helpline, is on 14416 (or 1-800-891-4416), available 24×7 in multiple languages. In an emergency, call 112. Please do not wait for a coaching session — reach out now.

Common questions

Are you a psychologist or therapist?
Our counsellors provide career and life-direction coaching. We do not offer clinical psychology or psychiatric services, and we do not diagnose or treat any condition. Where clinical support is appropriate, we refer you to a qualified, registered professional.
Is what I say confidential?
Yes. Session notes are private and are never shared without your written consent — the only exception being where the law requires disclosure or where there is a serious and immediate risk to someone's safety.
Can I book this for someone else?
You can pay for it, but the person must want it themselves and must book the free call with us directly. Coaching someone who was sent against their will does not work.
What if four sessions aren't enough?
You can extend session by session at the standard rate. There is no lock-in and no auto-renewal.
