NEET counselling, without the sales pitch

One score, dozens of paths, and a counselling calendar that punishes every day you hesitate. We help you and your family decide with facts — and we earn nothing from any college you choose.

Who this is for

If any of this sounds like your house right now

  • Result is out and the family cannot agree on repeat vs. admission
  • An agent has quoted a "management seat" and you don't know if it is fair
  • You don't know whether your rank stands a chance in later rounds
  • You want to stay in healthcare, but MBBS is not happening this year
  • You are a dropper deciding whether a second attempt is worth another year
Read this first: nobody can promise you a seat — not us, not any consultant. Seats are allotted by MCC and the state authorities purely on rank, category and choice order. Anyone guaranteeing admission for a fee is a warning sign, not a shortcut.

What the engagement includes

1. Score & rank reality check

Your marks, category, domicile and previous-year trends put side by side, so you know which rounds you are genuinely in the running for.

2. Round-by-round strategy

All India Quota, state quota, deemed and private — which to register for, what each costs, and how the free-exit / forfeiture rules affect you.

3. Choice list built with you

We construct and order your preference list together, and review it before you lock it from your own login.

4. The alternatives conversation

BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BUMS, BVSc, B.Sc. Nursing, physiotherapy, allied health, B.Pharm, biotech, paramedical and abroad options — with real career outcomes.

The repeat-year decision

A drop year costs money, a year of your life, and a real emotional toll. We work through it with a checklist instead of a slogan:

  • How much of your score gap is syllabus vs. exam-day execution?
  • What did your attempt-wise accuracy actually look like?
  • Is there a study environment at home or in the coaching that will change?
  • What is the family's financial and emotional runway for one more year?
  • What is the honest probability band — and what is the Plan B if it repeats?

Sometimes the answer is yes, repeat. Often it is not. We will tell you which one your own data points to.

Common questions

Will you fill my form or log in for me?
No. We guide you and review your list, but you or your parent always operate your own login. We will never ask for your password or OTP, and you should never share them with any consultant.
Do you have tie-ups with private medical colleges?
No. We take zero commission or referral fee from any institute. Our entire income is the fee you pay us, which is exactly why our shortlist can be honest about a college's fee, bond or quality.
My score is low. Is a session still worth it?
Often that is when it matters most, because the alternative options are the ones families know least about. But if we think we cannot add value, we will say so on the free call rather than take your money.
When should we book?
Ideally within a week of the result, before registration windows open. Late bookings are still useful, but your choices narrow with every round.
