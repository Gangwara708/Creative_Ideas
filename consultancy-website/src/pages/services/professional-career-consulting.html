Stop applying into the void

Four hundred applications and six calls is not bad luck — it is a targeting and positioning problem. We fix what you are applying for, what you are sending, and what you say when they finally call.

Who this is for

Freshers, stuck professionals and switchers

Freshers

Graduated, no calls, no clarity on which roles you are even eligible for.

2–8 years in

Growth has flattened, the work has gone stale, or the industry is shrinking.

Switchers & returners

Changing function or industry, or restarting after a break or a layoff.

Important: we are a career consulting practice, not a placement agency. We do not sell jobs, we do not charge candidates for referrals, and we will never ask you to pay a company for an offer. If anyone does, walk away.

What we work on

  1. Target definition

    Three to five specific role titles you are actually competitive for, with the companies and salary bands attached. Vague targets are why applications fail.

  2. Positioning & story

    How your existing experience maps to the target role, and how to explain a switch, a gap or a short stint without sounding defensive.

  3. Resume rebuild

    One page (two if experienced), ATS-parseable, achievement-led with numbers. You get the editable file, not a locked PDF.

  4. LinkedIn & sourcing

    Headline, About and skills rewritten for recruiter search, plus a weekly application routine: job boards, referrals, and direct outreach that gets replies.

  5. Skill-gap plan

    The two or three capabilities standing between you and the target role, with a realistic learning sequence — and an honest view of which certificates are worth the money.

  6. Interview preparation

    Mock interviews recorded and reviewed: your introduction, project deep-dives, behavioural answers, and the questions you should be asking them.

  7. Offer & negotiation

    How to read the CTC breakup, what is negotiable, how to compare two offers, and how to ask without souring the relationship.

Common questions

Do you find jobs for me or refer me to companies?
No. We make you a stronger, better-targeted candidate and teach you the search process. Any consultancy claiming a guaranteed placement for a fee should be treated with suspicion.
Will you write my resume for me?
We rebuild it with you in the session — your material, our structure and language. You leave with the editable file and the ability to update it yourself later.
I have a 2-year gap. Is that fatal?
No. It needs a clear, unapologetic explanation and evidence that you have stayed current. That is exactly the kind of thing we rehearse in the mock interview.
Can sessions happen after office hours?
Yes. Evening and Sunday slots are available for working professionals — say so when you book.
