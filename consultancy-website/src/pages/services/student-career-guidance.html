Choose the stream that fits the student

Science because the neighbour's son took science. Commerce because "science nahi chali". A ten-year path decided in a ten-minute conversation. There is a better way to do this — and it takes about two weeks.

The problem

Stream selection is the highest-leverage decision of school

Change it later and you lose a year at minimum. Get it wrong quietly and you lose interest, then marks, then confidence — and by Class 12 everyone assumes the student "isn't a hard worker". In our experience that diagnosis is usually wrong.

What the assessment covers

🧠

Aptitude

Numerical, verbal, spatial, abstract and mechanical reasoning — the capacities different streams actually draw on.

❤️

Interest

Where attention goes when nobody is forcing it. Interest predicts persistence better than early marks do.

🧩

Personality & work style

Structured vs. open-ended work, people-facing vs. solo, risk appetite — the fit factors that decide whether a career is survivable.

📈

Academic evidence

Subject-wise marks trend across the last two years, read alongside the test results rather than in isolation.

How it runs

  1. Free discovery call (15 min)

    With the parent and the student together. We explain the process and check that this is the right time to do it.

  2. Online assessment (60–75 min)

    Completed at home, in one sitting, on any laptop or phone. There is no pass or fail.

  3. Student session (60 min)

    We go through the results with the student first — alone. Honest answers need a room without judgement in it.

  4. Parent debrief (30–40 min)

    Findings, recommended stream and subject combinations, and how to support the plan at home without turning it into pressure.

  5. Written roadmap

    Stream, subject combination, board considerations, target entrance exams with timelines, and skill-building suggestions for the next two years.

For parents: we will never tell your child that your preferred option is stupid, and we will never tell you that your child's preference is a phase. Our job is to put evidence on the table so the two of you can stop arguing from opinion.

Common questions

Which class is the right time?
Class 9 or 10 is ideal — before the stream decision. Class 11 and 12 students still benefit, with the focus shifting to course and entrance-exam selection.
Is the test scientific?
We use standardised, published psychometric instruments and interpret them alongside academic and interview evidence. No single test decides anything — a report that claims to hand you one "correct" career is overselling itself.
Do you talk to the student alone?
Yes, for one session — with your consent, and with nothing clinical involved. Students say things about pressure and interest that they will not say in front of family, and that information changes the advice.
My child is under 18. What about consent and data?
A parent or legal guardian must book, give consent and be present for the debrief. Assessment data is used only for the counselling and is deleted on request. See our privacy policy.
