Should you go abroad at all? Let's answer that first.

Most study-abroad "counselling" in India is university commission work wearing a counsellor's badge. We charge you a fee and take nothing from any institution — so the first honest question is whether the ₹35–60 lakh is worth it in your case.

How the industry usually works: many agencies earn a commission from the universities they place you in. That is legal, but it quietly shapes the shortlist you are shown. Ask any consultant — including us — this question: "who pays you?" You are entitled to a straight answer.

What we do

  1. The go / don't-go analysis

    Your profile, finances and goal compared against the domestic alternative. For some, a master's in India plus two years of work is the better bet — and we will say so.

  2. Country & course shortlisting

    Academic fit, cost of living, part-time work rules, post-study work duration and immigration climate — across the UK, USA, Canada, Germany, Australia, Ireland, Netherlands, Singapore, UAE and others.

  3. The full-cost model

    Tuition, living, insurance, travel, visa and dependency costs modelled over the whole programme, against realistic post-study salary bands — including the tax and repayment reality.

  4. Funding plan

    Scholarships you actually qualify for, education loan structures, collateral vs. non-collateral, co-applicant requirements and interest impact over the full tenure.

  5. Application & documents

    Timeline per intake, test planning (IELTS/TOEFL/PTE, GRE/GMAT where needed), plus SOP, LOR and CV review — we edit your draft, we do not write it for you.

  6. Pre-departure briefing

    Visa documentation, accommodation, banking, and the practical things nobody tells first-time students until they are already there.

Scope note: we provide guidance and document review. We are not a visa consultancy, we do not lodge visa applications on your behalf, and we do not act as an overseas recruitment or placement agent. Immigration decisions rest entirely with the relevant government authority.

Questions we will make you answer

  • What is the specific job you expect on the other side, and who hires for it?
  • If you had to come back after the degree, would it still have been worth it?
  • Can the family fund the first year without a loan, and the loan without the family?
  • Is this course a genuine specialisation, or a repackaged general degree?
  • What happens to your plan if the post-study work rules change next year?

Common questions

Do you take commission from universities?
No. We charge the client a fee and accept nothing from any institution. If that ever changes, it will be disclosed on this page in plain language.
Do you guarantee admission or a visa?
No. Admissions are decided by universities and visas by governments. We improve the quality of your application and the realism of your shortlist.
Will you write my SOP?
No — and you should be wary of anyone who offers to. Universities are increasingly good at spotting purchased or AI-generated statements. We give you structure, questions and detailed edits on your own draft.
How early should we start?
Ten to twelve months before your intended intake. Later is possible but it narrows scholarship options and loan processing time.
