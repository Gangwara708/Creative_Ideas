Your rank is fixed. Your choice list is not.

Two students with the same rank routinely end up in very different places — because one of them ordered the choice list properly. This is the part of JEE that is still in your control, and it takes a few hours of clear thinking.

Who this is for

JEE Main and Advanced candidates, and their parents

  • You have a rank and a JoSAA window opening in days
  • You are torn between a "better branch" and a "better name"
  • You are weighing NIT vs. IIIT vs. GFTI vs. state government vs. private
  • Your family wants CS at any cost and you are not sure that is right
  • You are considering a drop year and want it analysed, not cheered on

How we build your choice list

  1. Fix the eligibility set

    Category, home state, gender-neutral vs. female-supernumerary pools, and which counselling bodies you must register with separately.

  2. Map rank to a probability band

    Using opening and closing ranks across recent years and rounds, we sort institutes into likely / stretch / safe — not a single-year snapshot.

  3. Order by genuine preference, not by cutoff

    The algorithm rewards honest ordering. We make you rank what you actually want, then check the list for the classic ordering mistakes.

  4. Plan the rounds

    Float, slide and freeze decisions, seat-acceptance fees, withdrawal deadlines and the CSAB special round — decided in advance, not in a panic.

Branch vs. college: the trade-off, made explicit

There is no universal answer, but there is a structured way to reach yours. We look at four things together:

Placement reality

Branch-wise placement percentages and median (not headline) packages from official reports — including how many offers came from mass recruiters.

Branch-change rules

Whether the institute allows a change after the first year, and the CGPA you would realistically need.

What you'll survive

Four years of a subject you dislike is a real cost. Aptitude and interest data matter here, not just cutoffs.

Total cost & location

Fees, hostel, travel and the scholarship or fee-waiver schemes you may qualify for.

The honest version: for most careers, the branch you can stay interested in beats the brand you were pushed into. But if your target is a specific core industry or a research path, the institute's lab and recruiter ecosystem genuinely matters. Which is why the answer has to be yours, not a rule.

Beyond JoSAA

If your rank does not open the doors you hoped for, there are more paths than the internet suggests. We cover state counselling, private and deemed universities worth their fee, BITS/VIT/SRM-type exams still open, integrated M.Tech and dual degrees, design and pure-science options, and the realistic route through a lesser-known college to a strong master's programme.

Common questions

Can you guarantee a seat in a particular NIT or IIT?
No, and nobody can. Allotment is a function of your rank, category and the list you submit. We can improve the quality of the list; we cannot change the arithmetic.
How many choices should I fill?
Almost always more than you think, in honest preference order. The most common self-inflicted damage we see is a short list that runs out before the rounds do.
Is a drop year worth it for JEE?
Sometimes. We look at your paper-wise analysis, how much of the syllabus was genuinely covered, your improvement trend in mocks, and the family's capacity to support another year. Then we give you a straight answer.
Do you charge per round?
No. The admission-season package covers the full season including follow-up calls after each allotment. See pricing.
